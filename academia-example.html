Bio

About Me

Hi! I am Jikai Sun, a PhD student in Mass Communication at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, minoring in Computer Science (expected to graduate in 2028). My research interests focus on health communication, the mechanisms underlying the spread of misinformation, and the roles, applications, and impacts of emerging technologies such as large language models in communication processes. I mainly use experimental and survey-based approaches to conduct my studies, and I also have a strong interest in other causal inference methods as well as computational social science. Now, I am a team member of the NSF-DST Team led by Jaideep Srivastava and Emily Vraga, and also a research assistant in the Computational Multi-Modal Communication Lab led by Cuihua Shen, Yilang Peng, and Yingdan Lu.

I am now advised by Dr. Emily Vraga. I obtained my M.A. at Renmin University of China. My advisor was Dr. Wenjing Pan during my master’s program, and we have collaborated on multiple research projects regarding health communication, misinformation, gaming, and AI. I received my B.A. from Jinan University, where I was advised by Dr. Tao Liu. My works have been published in international journals such as Health Communication, International Journal of Communication, and Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking, as well as leading Chinese communication journals, including Journal of Journalism & Communication Studies (新闻与传播研究), and Chinese Journal of Journalism & Communication (国际新闻界). My works have also been presented at international conferences, like EMNLP (2025), ICA (2023, 2025, 2026), and IAMCR (2026).
